1 Introduction & Who We Are

TastyKart ("we", "our", or "us") operates a food-delivery platform consisting of:

  • A customer mobile application (Android — package: com.arrowcoders.foodapp)
  • A delivery-partner mobile application (Android)
  • A web-based admin panel (tastykart-landing.web.app)

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use any part of our Service. By using TastyKart, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and profile photo provided during registration or via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0).
  • Location data — precise GPS coordinates when you use the app to find nearby restaurants or to enable delivery tracking. Location is collected only while the app is in active use and only with your permission.
  • Order data — items ordered, delivery address, payment method type, order history, and Razorpay transaction reference IDs. We never store full card numbers or CVV codes.
  • Device information — device model, OS version, Firebase installation ID, and push-notification token (FCM) for order-status alerts.
  • Usage data — screens viewed, search queries, filter selections, and feature interactions, collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the Service.
  • Communications — messages you send to our support team via email.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your identity.
  • Process and fulfil food orders, including communicating order status.
  • Calculate delivery fees based on your location and distance.
  • Send push notifications and in-app alerts for order updates.
  • Enable live delivery tracking.
  • Improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations.
  • Respond to your customer-support requests.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising or sell it to third parties.

4 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Restaurant partners — order details (items, special instructions, delivery address) are shared with the restaurant fulfilling your order.
  • Delivery partners — your first name and delivery address are shared with the assigned delivery partner to complete your delivery.
  • Razorpay (Payment Processor) — payment is handled entirely by Razorpay's secure checkout. TastyKart receives only a masked payment reference and transaction status. Razorpay's Privacy Policy governs their data handling.
  • Google Firebase — our entire backend runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Firebase Analytics, and Hosting). Data is stored in Google's data centers. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or a governmental authority in India.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5 Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage, hosted in Google's data centres. We implement the following safeguards:

  • Firebase Security Rules restricting data access by authenticated user identity.
  • HTTPS-only data transmission for all API and app communications.
  • Server-side payment verification via HMAC-SHA256 signatures (never trusting client-side results).
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for all admin operations.
  • Customer block/fraud detection at the checkout flow.

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Order history is retained for a minimum of 3 years for legal, tax, and compliance purposes.
  • Location data is not stored beyond the active delivery session.
  • Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics default retention settings (up to 14 months).
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time (see Section 7).

7 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated personal data.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw location permission at any time via your device settings. Note: this may affect core delivery functionality.
  • Opt out of push notifications — via your device notification settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@tastykart.in. We will respond within 30 days.

8 Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without parental consent, please contact us at support@tastykart.in and we will delete that information promptly.

9 Third-Party Services

TastyKart integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Razorpay — razorpay.com/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform — used for delivery address selection and distance calculation.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
  • Sending an in-app notification for material changes.

Continued use of the Service after the effective date of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
